# Capture Micro Journal

Date: 2026-09-10

Tiny app concept: a warm command center **micro journal**.

Core touches:

- daily streak tracking
- progress rings
- keyboard shortcuts

Palette:

- Background: `#262322`
- Accent: `#ddc9b4`
- Text: `#f0f7ee`
